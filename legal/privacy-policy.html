Privacy Policy

Modern Dairy — Business & Personal Ordering App
Last updated: 15 August 2026

Modern Dairy ("we", "us", "our") operates the Modern Dairy mobile application (the "App") for ordering dairy, frozen, and beverage products for business and personal customers in Pune, India. This policy explains what information we collect through the App, how we use it, and who we share it with.

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhatWhy
Account detailsName, mobile number, password (stored as a hash, never in plain text)To create and secure your account and identify you when you sign in
Business details (Business accounts only)GSTIN, registered business name, address on GST recordsTo verify eligibility for wholesale pricing and issue correct tax invoices
Order & delivery informationItems ordered, quantities, delivery address, order historyTo fulfil and track your orders, and let you reorder easily
Payment & wallet informationWallet balance, transaction history, payment confirmation from our payment processorTo process top-ups and deduct order payments. We do not store your card, UPI, or bank details ourselves — these are handled directly by our payment processor (Razorpay)
CommunicationsSupport messages you send us (phone, WhatsApp, email)To respond to your questions or complaints

2. How we verify your phone number

When you sign up or sign in, we send a one-time password (OTP) to your mobile number through our SMS verification partner. We only use this to confirm the number belongs to you — we do not use it for any other purpose.

3. Third parties we share information with

We use the following service providers to operate the App. Each only receives the information it needs to perform its specific function:

  • SMS/OTP verification provider — receives your mobile number to send and verify the OTP code.
  • GST verification provider — receives the GSTIN you enter (Business accounts) to confirm it against government GST records.
  • Payment processor (Razorpay) — receives payment details you enter directly with them to process wallet top-ups; we never see or store your raw payment credentials.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers (Google Cloud / Firebase) — host our backend systems and databases.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not share it with advertisers.

4. Data retention

We keep your account and order information for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as required for tax, accounting, and legal record-keeping (typically up to 8 years for financial records, in line with Indian tax law).

5. Your rights

You can:

  • Review and update your account details in the App (Account tab).
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated personal data, subject to records we're legally required to retain (e.g. completed order/tax records).

To exercise any of these, contact us using the details in Section 8.

6. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data in transit, hashed password storage, and rate limiting to prevent abuse of our systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information appropriately.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for business owners and adult personal customers. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.
